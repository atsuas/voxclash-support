English

1. Operator and scope

This policy describes how EGG BASE LAB handles information in Vox Clash and when you contact us. The game does not require an EGG BASE LAB account. We do not ask players to enter their name, postal address, telephone number or email address in the game. This policy does not cover other apps published by EGG BASE LAB.

2. Game data stored on your device

Progress, coins, lives, unlocked animals and items, room layouts, preferences and purchase entitlements are stored on your device. We do not operate a server that receives these saved games. Device or operating-system backups may retain app data according to your settings. Clearing app data or uninstalling generally removes local saves; it does not cancel purchases or remove records retained by the app store or advertising providers.

3. Advertising and technical information

Vox Clash integrates Unity LevelPlay, ironSource Ads, Unity Ads and Google AdMob, together with Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) for privacy choices. Depending on platform, region, permissions and privacy choices, these providers may process IP addresses, advertising and other device identifiers, device and OS details, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, ad impressions and interactions, app interaction signals, and diagnostics. We do not request access to contacts, photos, microphone or precise GPS location for gameplay.

Advertising information is used to deliver and measure ads, administer rewarded ads, control frequency, detect fraud, and diagnose technical problems. Where permitted by your choices and applicable requirements, advertising providers may use information for personalized advertising and combine it with information they hold from other services. Their policies describe their own processing and retention.

  • Unity / ironSource Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

4. Consent, tracking and your choices

Where required, regional privacy messages explain advertising choices. You can open the game’s privacy options from Settings to review available choices; the options shown depend on region and provider requirements. On iOS, permission to track across other companies’ apps and websites is requested through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. Declining that permission does not prevent gameplay and is separate from regional advertising consent.

You can play without consenting to optional advertising processing. When the required consent or other eligibility conditions are not met, advertising features, including rewarded videos, may be unavailable. You can also use your device’s privacy settings. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by the applicable app store. We do not receive payment-card numbers or your billing address. The app processes product identifiers, transaction identifiers and verified purchase or entitlement information to grant items, avoid duplicate grants and restore eligible purchases. Payment and transaction records maintained by the store are subject to its privacy policy. Consumable items and non-consumable purchases may have different restoration rules.

  • Unity / ironSource Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

6. Children and families

Vox Clash is intended for a mixed audience that includes adults and children, rather than primarily for children under 13. Parents and guardians should supervise children’s use of the game, advertising features and in-app purchases, and may use the device’s parental controls. We do not ask children to send personal information through the game.

Advertising services can process device information as described above; the presence of a child-friendly character does not mean that no data is processed. If you believe a child’s personal information has been collected or used inappropriately, contact us so that we can investigate and, where appropriate, seek deletion or coordinate with the relevant provider. Do not send a child’s full birth date, payment details or other unnecessary personal information in a support request.

7. Support inquiries

If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents and attachments you choose to send. We use this information to answer questions, resolve problems and handle privacy requests. Please send only information necessary for the inquiry. Support correspondence is retained only as needed for these purposes and applicable recordkeeping obligations.

8. Sharing, retention and international processing

Advertising providers and their demand partners process information to provide their services. App stores process purchases, and email providers process support correspondence. We do not directly sell personal information. Some advertising-provider activity may qualify as sale, sharing or targeted advertising under local law; applicable opt-out choices depend on your region and the service.

Third-party records are retained under the providers’ policies and applicable obligations. Local game data remains until removed or replaced, subject to backups. Providers may process data outside your country. See their policies for retention, transfer safeguards and privacy-request channels.

9. Your rights and security

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data, object to processing, withdraw consent or opt out of certain advertising uses, and complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us using the address below. As there is no EGG BASE LAB game account, we may need limited information to identify relevant support records or help direct a request to a provider. Do not send passwords or full payment details.

We use platform security features and limit the information requested for support. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The date above indicates the latest revision.
